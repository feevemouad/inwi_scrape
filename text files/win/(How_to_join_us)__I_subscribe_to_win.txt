https://win.ma/en/faq/i-create-my-win-account-facebook-615
Question: I create my win account with Facebook
Answer:   If you want to create a win account using your Facebook account, just click on the button I create my account with Facebook on the Account creation page. Your name, first name and email address (possibly phone number, if it is your Facebook ID) as well as your photo are recovered.
 
 If you have entered your email address on Facebook, it will be automatically pre-filled, you will only have to confirm it.
 
 If you have not entered an email address on Facebook, you will need to enter it.
 
 You will then receive a confirmation email to validate the creation of your account and will have 48 hours to confirm your email address.
 
 Once your account creation validated, you can start your subscription or continue it if you already have a cart in progress.
