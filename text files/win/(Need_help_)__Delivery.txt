Question: I still haven't received my SIM
Answer:   You placed your order more than 4 working days ago *, but you have not yet received your win SIM card? Don't worry, it's on its way. Start by checking the status of your delivery: Log in to your account and go to the “my orders [LINK: https://win.ma/espace-client/commandes]” section. In the event of a delivery problem, contact the carrier directly. If the carrier cannot help you, contact a win advisor [LINK: https://win.ma/faq/comment-echanger-avec-win-655] during our business hours so we can help you resolve this issue. 
 * Excluding weekends and public holidays
