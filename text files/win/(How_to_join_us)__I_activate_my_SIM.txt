Question: I received my win SIM and I want to activate my offer
Answer:   Once you received your SIM card and insert it into your smartphone, you can activate your offer directly from your dashboard via the Activate my offer section. 
 The activation of your offer is immediate and its renewal is done the same day every month.

Question: How do I scan my ICC ID?
Answer:   To activate your package, please have your win SIM card with you.
 
 After clicking on "Yes, I have a win SIM card", scan using the camera tool on your phone or enter the last 11 digits of your ICC identifier on the back of your new SIM card as well as your Puk code.

Question: How do I sign my win contract?
Answer:   To sign your win contract, nothing could be simpler.
 
 After having inserted your win SIM, all you have to do is go to your win account and click on "Activate my offer" then "Validate my contract".

Question: I have ordered a new SIM card, how do I activate it?
Answer:   Once you have received your new SIM card, go to your customer area and click on the red banner to be redirected to the current activation screen: Scan or enter the last 11 digits of your ICC ID on the back of your new SIM card and your PUK code. Check the information you have entered and click on "I confirm".   Congratulations! You have successfully activated your win SIM card. You can now fully enjoy your new win by inwi plan !

Question: How do I activate an inwi SIM card ?
Answer:   To activate your inwi SIM card as part of an after-sales service:
 
  -Go to your customer area, click on the Manage my SIM section
 
 - Choose to activate a new inwi SIM and then click on "I activate a new inwi SIM"
 
 - Enter the phone number of the inwi SIM card you wish to use and then click on "I continue"
 
 - You will then receive a validation code which you must enter and then click on "I continue". 
 
 Congratulations! You have successfully activated your win SIM card.
