Question: I cannot create my account
Answer:   If you're having trouble creating your account, you can do the following:  Is the format of the email address entered correct? Is the password entered secure and does it have 8 characters including 1 letter, 1 number and 1 special character? Does the email address entered not correspond to an account already created? If you still cannot create your account, do not hesitate to contact our advisers.
