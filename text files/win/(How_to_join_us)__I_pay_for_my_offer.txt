Question: How do we pay on win?
Answer:   On win, we accept the following payments: 
 - Payment via inwi money: a mobile payment account that allows you to instantly pay your win mobile plan and pass using only your mobile number. More info on inwimoney.ma
 
 - Payment by Moroccan credit card: If you choose to pay by credit card, you will have direct access to a secure screen to enter your card details and validate your payment. win will not have access to any payment data.
 
 - Payment with your application or banking site: This method of payment allows you to finalize your transactions on your banking interface within 48 hours thanks to a payment code that you will find on your Customer Area, My Payments, Payments in progress.
 
 Our banking partners on website/mobile application: Attijari Wafa Bank, Banque populaire, Société générale, Barid Bank, Crédit agricole, CIH Bank. More information in video by clicking here [LINK: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rSO81tJfSws]

Question: I pay using my banking application / website
Answer:   Before choosing the payment from your banking application or website, check that your bank offers this service. 
 Our banking partners on website/mobile application: Attijari Wafa Bank, Banque populaire, Société générale, Barid Bank, Crédit agricole, CIH Bank.
 
 
 When you reach the payment stage, choose "Payment on app / bank site" from the payment methods offered. A payment code will be displayed in your dashboard, My Payments, during payment with the total amount to be paid.
 
 You just need to enter this code on your banking interface in the space reserved as soon as it is displayed and within a maximum of 48 hours. Once the payment has been made, you will find your payment receipt in your dashboard, My Payments, Payment Receipts.
 
 Beyond 48 hours, your payment code will have expired.
 
 To complete your purchase, you will have to proceed to payment again.   More information in video by clicking here [LINK: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rSO81tJfSws]

Question: Can I pay cash for my win plan?
Answer:   Win is a 100% digital universe. All payments are made securely online. Win offers you the possibility to pay for your win package without a credit card from the inwi money application. With inwi money your phone becomes your mean of payment. Recharging your inwi money balance and making payments becomes simple, immediate and secure. To benefit from it, all you need to do is create an inwi money customer account and recharge it with cash or by credit card in one of the inwi branches.    I do not have an inwi money customer account:  To start paying for your win package without a bank card, you must have an inwi money customer account. To do this, just follow the following steps: a. Download the inwi money application from Google Play or the App Store regardless of your operator. You can also dial * 800 # from an inwi mobile and follow the subscription steps. You can subscribe with your current number which will be ported to win. b. Go to the nearest authorized inwi money point of sale and deposit the amount of money you want. c. Use inwi Money to pay for your win package  [LINK: https://win.ma/faq/je-paie-en-utilisant-inwi-money-587] You can carry out your transactions immediately and safely. You can also withdraw your money in cash at the nearest authorized inwi money point of sale.
