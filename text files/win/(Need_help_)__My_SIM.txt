Question: I lost my SIM
Answer:   If you have lost your SIM card and want to order a new one, you can do so from your win account via the "Manage my SIM" section and then by clicking on "Order a new SIM". Just follow the steps and we will send it to the delivery address of your choice as soon as possible.
