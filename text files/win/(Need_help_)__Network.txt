Question: I can't get the network
Answer:   Are the reception bars at the top of your smartphone screen not displayed?

Question: I cannot make calls / SMS
Answer:   If you cannot make a call or send an SMS, you can check the following:
 
 Network coverage 
 To check if you are in a covered area, you can consult the national 2G / 3G / 4G coverage map and enter the address where you are located.
 
 Destination of calls and SMS Calls to national numbers:
 check that you have a call balance (mobile plan or Pass) which allows you to make national calls. International calls: check that you have a call balance (Pass) that allows you to make international calls. Roaming calls: check that you have a call balance (Pass) which allows you to make / receive calls in Roaming. SMS in Roaming, SMS to international, SMS / Calls to short numbers: check that you have respectively a Roaming, international or value-added services balance that allows you to perform these operations. And if you still cannot resolve the problem, please do not hesitate to contact our advisers for assistance.  

https://win.ma/en/faq/i-have-trouble-hearing-my-interlocutor-573
Question: I have trouble hearing my interlocutor
Answer:   Can't hear the other party's voice clearly, or just hear unwanted noises when you call? In this case, carry out the following checks:
 
 Network coverage
 To check if you are in a covered area, you can consult the national 2G / 3G / 4G coverage map and enter the address where you are located.
 
 Smartphone settings
 If you are in a covered area and the problem persists, you can check the settings of your smartphone: When you make a call, check that the volume level of your phone is at its maximum. To avoid noise and crackling, make sure that your phone's receiver is cleaned and is not clogged with dust or debris. If your phone is protected by a case or a shell, check that the receiver of your phone is not obstructed by this accessory. If the quality of your calls is still as bad as this, do not hesitate to contact our advisers for assistance.  

https://win.ma/en/faq/my-internet-connection-speed-slow-575
Question: My Internet connection speed is slow
Answer:   By performing the checks below, you will be able to determine why the speed of your connection is low:
 
 Network coverage 
 To check if you are in a covered area, we invite you to consult the national 2G / 3G / 4G coverage map and to fill in the address where you are located.
 
 Smartphone settings
 If you are in a covered area and the problem persists, make sure you are in 4G. If you are not, you can change the Internet settings of your smartphone:
 
 For iPhone 
 Select "Settings", then "Cellular data" On "Options", select "Activate 4G" and finally choose "Voice and data"
 
 For Android 
 Select "Settings" then "Connections" then "Mobile networks" In "Network Mode", change your cellular data settings to "4G / 3G / 2G" And if you still cannot resolve the problem, please do not hesitate to contact our advisers for assistance.

Question: I can't configure 4G
Answer:   Generally, if the win SIM card is inserted in a 4G compatible smartphone, 4G is activated automatically.  
 
 Then make sure that the area where you are is well covered in 4G.
 
 If 4G does not activate automatically the first time you use your SIM win in a phone, an SMS to configure network parameters (APN) is sent to you.
 
 You will then have to enter the code "0000" for the configuration to be taken into account with the new APN: www.inwi.ma
 
 Once the APN has been set up, the smartphone should display 4G or LTE at the top of the screen next to the network signal bars: this means that the smartphone is receiving a 4G Internet network.
 
 If you have not received the connection parameters, you can send 3 to 333 (Free SMS), you will receive an SMS with the configuration parameters of your smartphone. And if you still cannot configure 4G on your phone, proceed to the manual configuration of your smartphone:
 
 Manual configuration of 4G on iOS - Versions 12 
 At the level of "Settings": Select "Cellular data" then "Options" then choose "Cellular data network".
 
 If "Cellular Data Network" is not displayed, manual configuration is not possible.
 In this case, connect the device to the computer and use iTunes to install "Operator Settings". Fill in the following fields: On cellular data Access point name: www.inwi.ma
 
 On MMS Access point name: www.inwi.ma Manual configuration of 4G on iOS - Old versions: At the level of "Settings":

 On cellular data
