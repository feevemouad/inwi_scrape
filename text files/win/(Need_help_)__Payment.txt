Question: My credit card payment failed
Answer:   If you cannot finalize your payment using your Moroccan bank card or your international bank card, we advise you to try again a second time or to make the payment with another bank card. If the problem persists, we invite you to contact your bank. In the meantime, you can finalize your purchase using the other payment methods offered.

Question: I didn't pay on time and I was suspended
Answer:   Was your win invoice not paid on time? We have sent you several reminders on this subject. To benefit from our services again, simply renew your plan. Note that :
 
 Reception of calls is maintained after suspension.
 
 If you have a valid Pass, you can still continue to use it until it expires.
 
 After suspension for non-payment, you will no longer be able to purchase a Pass.
 
 After 12 months of suspension, without any action from your end, your line will be automatically terminated. You can nevertheless reactivate your offer at any time, either by keeping your previous plan, or by configuring a new plan.
